7 Types of Text Case Conversions

1. UPPERCASE Converter

Convert any text to uppercase instantly. Perfect for headings, emphasis, or when you need all capital letters.

2. lowercase Converter

Transform uppercase to lowercase with one click. Fix accidental caps lock or standardize your text.

3. Title Case Converter

Our title case converter capitalizes the first letter of every major word. Ideal for book titles and headlines.

4. Sentence Case Converter

The sentence case converter only capitalizes the first letter of the first word - just like standard English sentences.

5. Capitalized Case Converter

Every word's first letter becomes uppercase. Great for name lists and UI buttons.

6. Alternating Case Converter

Creates a pattern of uppercase and lowercase letters (aLtErNaTiNg). Perfect for creative writing.

7. Inverse Case Converter

Flips every character's case - uppercase becomes lowercase and vice versa.
