🧠 How Different Cases Work

UPPERCASE: Converts every letter to capital letters. Ideal for emphasis or acronyms.
lowercase: All letters become small, perfect for cleaning up text.
Title Case: Capitalizes main words (useful for headlines, book titles).
Sentence case: Only the first letter of the first word is capitalized (standard for sentences).
Capitalized Case: Every word's first letter capitalized.
aLtErNaTiNg: Fun pattern of alternating case.
iNVERSE cASE: Reverses the current case of each character.
